# ApplyWise AI — Ethics and Responsible Use

## Purpose

ApplyWise AI is a résumé and job-description comparison tool designed to help
job seekers understand how their documented skills align with a role.

It is a learning and decision-support application. It is not a recruitment,
screening, ranking, or hiring system.

## Intended Use

ApplyWise may be used to:

- Identify matched skills
- Identify missing required or preferred skills
- Review experience and education requirements side by side
- Understand how the technical match score was calculated
- Help users improve the clarity and relevance of their applications

The results should support human judgment rather than replace it.

## Prohibited Use

ApplyWise should not be used to:

- Automatically accept or reject candidates
- Rank candidates for employment
- Make final hiring decisions
- Determine interview eligibility
- Make claims about a person's intelligence, personality, honesty, or potential
- Infer sensitive or protected characteristics
- Discriminate based on age, gender, race, religion, disability, nationality,
  marital status, or another protected characteristic

## Technical Score

The technical score is an estimated comparison between extracted résumé skills
and extracted job-description skills.

The score:

- Is not an employer-issued score
- Does not represent a candidate's complete ability
- Does not measure communication, teamwork, motivation, or potential
- Depends on the quality of the uploaded résumé
- Depends on the accuracy of AI-assisted extraction
- May not reflect synonyms or experience expressed indirectly
- Does not guarantee selection, rejection, or interview eligibility

When both required and preferred skills are available:

- Required skills contribute 80% of the score
- Preferred skills contribute 20% of the score

When only one skill group is available, that group determines the score.

## Experience and Education

ApplyWise displays candidate and job information side by side.

It does not automatically determine whether the candidate fully satisfies the
experience or education requirements.

Users must review these sections manually.

## AI Limitations

ApplyWise uses a language model to extract structured information from résumés
and job descriptions.

The model may:

- Miss a skill that is present
- Extract a skill that is not clearly supported
- Misinterpret experience duration
- Misclassify required and preferred skills
- Interpret education requirements incorrectly
- Produce incomplete structured results

All extracted information should be reviewed by the user.

## Fairness

Résumés differ in format, language, writing quality, industry terminology, and
access to professional guidance.

A lower score may reflect documentation or extraction limitations rather than a
candidate's true ability.

ApplyWise should not penalize users because of résumé style, background, writing
format, or access to career resources.

Protected and sensitive personal characteristics must not be intentionally used
in technical scoring.

## Transparency

The application should clearly show:

- Matched skills
- Missing skills
- Required and preferred skills
- Score weighting
- Experience information
- Education information
- Relevant limitations and disclaimers

Users should be able to understand why a score was produced.

## Privacy

Résumé and job-description text may be processed by third-party services such as
Groq.

Optional feedback may be stored through Supabase.

Users should not upload unnecessary sensitive or confidential information.

Further details are available in:

[Data Boundary and Privacy](data_boundary.md)

## Human Review

Users should verify the results against the original résumé and job description.

No important employment or career decision should be made solely from the
ApplyWise score or extracted output.

## Disclaimer

ApplyWise provides an estimated comparison based only on the uploaded résumé and
the provided job description.

It does not guarantee selection, rejection, interview eligibility, employment,
or hiring outcomes.
